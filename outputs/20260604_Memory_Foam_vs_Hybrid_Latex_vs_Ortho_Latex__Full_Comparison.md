### Introduction to Memory Foam, Hybrid Latex, and Ortho Latex Mattresses

Memory Foam mattresses are like a warm hug for your body. They conform to your shape, providing excellent pressure relief and support. They're made from a type of polyurethane foam that's sensitive to temperature and pressure, allowing it to mold to your body. Hybrid Latex mattresses, on the other hand, are like the best of both worlds. They combine latex with other materials, like memory foam or innerspring coils, for a balanced sleep experience. Ortho Latex mattresses are designed to provide superior support and pressure relief, making them perfect for people with back pain or other orthopedic issues.
